01 · What I do

Engineer by foundation. Integrator by practice. Director by responsibility.

Most integrators split engineering, sales and product between different people. I cover all three, so the person who scopes a system is the same person who knows how it will be built, run and supported.

PILLAR 01

Projects & engineering

Designing and delivering command, control and monitoring infrastructure, permanent or mobile.

  • Video walls, visualisation controllers and KVM operator workstations
  • HLD/LLD, rack design, signal flow, power and heat budgets
  • Encoders, decoders, IP video transport and recording sizing
  • Lab validation, commissioning and service lifecycle
PILLAR 02

Turning a customer's operational problem into an architecture, then proving it with a working demo before the meeting.

  • Datasheets, brochures and promo video

02 · Sectors & confidentiality

Built for clients who don't publish their work

Most of my work is delivered to security, defence and intelligence organisations. These systems carry live operational video, sensor data and command communications, so the people who build them are expected to be discreet.

CONFIDENTIAL BY DESIGN

Why this page is deliberately general

Security-sector clients are described by sector, not by name. Nothing on this page reveals:

  • Site locations, floor plans or system drawings
  • Network addresses, topologies, frequencies or credentials
  • Client specifications, capability gaps or quantities
  • Procurement, contract or programme details

How sensitive work is handled

  • Need-to-know sharing, and work carried out under client confidentiality agreements
  • Systems that run entirely on internal government networks and private cloud, with no internet exposure
  • Isolated links: private APN, IPSec and air-gapped KVM access to sensitive systems
  • Demos use simulated data. Public versions carry company branding, not client identities
  • Security patching across installed systems (e.g. a CVE remediation campaign for existing customers)

06 · Working method

How I move a project forward

I start with the operator's actual mission and work backward to a system that can be built, tested, supported and scaled.

STEP 01

Define the mission

User workflows, operational risks, required outcomes and environmental constraints.

STEP 02

Architect

Devices, transport, processing, applications, interfaces, redundancy and security boundaries.

STEP 03

Prove it

A working demo or lab build: connectivity, throughput and integration trials.

STEP 04

Engineer delivery

Vendors, BOM, site and network prerequisites, installation sequence and acceptance criteria.

STEP 05

Support & iterate

Commissioning, training, patching, and feeding field lessons into the next product revision.
